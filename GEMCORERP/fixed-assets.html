How is this calculated?
  • Fixed assets are separate from inventory. Any item tagged with an asset type is excluded from Inventory Value, Total Items, and the stock/aging/weekly reports. They only appear here.
  • Automatic: the tag is on the item, so when an MRR is made for a tagged item it lands here — nothing to do in the MRR.
  • Acquired Value = MRR received quantity × item unit cost, within the selected period (MRR date). On-Hand is the quantity currently recorded for the item.
  • Repairs and Maintenance is not automatically a fixed asset. Each R&M item is tagged Capital (major repair / adds to the useful life of the building → counted in Total Fixed Assets) or Expense (minor repair → shown separately, not counted). Default is Expense.
  • MRR history before November 2026 is not item-level in the live ledger, so Acquired Value covers live MRRs from Nov 2026.
