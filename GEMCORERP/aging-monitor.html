How is this calculated?
  • Aging is the number of whole months since an item was last issued (MRIF). The current month counts as 0.
  • Source: the same monthly data used by Monthly Movement — the legacy sheet for Jan–Oct 2026, plus the live system from Nov 2026. Data starts in Jan 2026, so the oldest age that can be shown is about 10–11 months.
  • Active: issued this month or last month. Slow: 2–3 months ago. Aging: 4–6 months ago. Dormant: 7 or more months ago. No Usage: never issued since the data starts.
  • New Stock: received in the last 2 months and not issued recently, so it is not counted as idle yet.
  • Stock Value = on-hand × unit cost. Items with no stock are hidden unless you tick “Include items with no stock”.
  • Months are whole months, so “Last Issued: Oct 2026” means sometime in October.
