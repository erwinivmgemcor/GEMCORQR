How is this calculated?
  • Age = number of days from the MRR (receipt) date until today. It answers: how long has the stock we hold now been in the warehouse?
  • FIFO: the oldest stock is assumed to be used first. So the stock on hand is taken from the newest receipts, counted back until it adds up to the on-hand quantity. Each receipt “layer” gets its own age and value.
  • Example: on-hand 10. Received 6 on Oct 20 and 8 on Aug 5 → 6 units are aged from Oct 20 and 4 units from Aug 5.
  • Receipt data: Jan–Oct 2026 from the MRR sheet (exact dates); Nov 2026 onward from live MRR transactions in the system.
  • Pre-2026: if the on-hand is more than everything received in 2026, the extra was already in stock before the sheet starts. Its exact age is unknown, but it is at least as old as the first 2026 receipt, so it is counted as the oldest group.
  • Age buckets: Fresh 0–30 days • Normal 31–90 • Aging 91–180 • Old 181+ • Pre-2026. “Over 90 Days” = Aging + Old + Pre-2026.
  • Status of an item = the age bucket of its oldest stock still on hand. Aged Value shows how much of the item's value is older than 90 days. Age Mix shows the split.
  • Stock Value = on-hand × unit cost. Items with no stock are hidden unless you tick “Include items with no stock”.
  • Last Issued comes from Monthly Movement (month only) and is for reference — it is not used to compute age.
